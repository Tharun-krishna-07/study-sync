# 🎓 StudySync

### Learn Together. Stay Connected. Grow Together.

**StudySync** is a collaborative learning platform that blends Discord-style community spaces with real study tools — live presence, scheduled sessions, an AI tutor that actually checks if you understood the material, and a gamified XP system that rewards genuine effort, not clicks.

🔗 **Live app:** [studysyncz.lovable.app](https://studysyncz.lovable.app/)

---

## ✨ Why StudySync?

Studying alone is boring. Studying in a chaotic group chat is worse. StudySync sits in between — it gives your study group a *persistent home*: servers, channels, live rooms, and an AI that keeps everyone honest about what they've actually learned.

---

## 🚀 Features

### 🟢 Tune In — No Pressure
Study independently while staying visibly present to your group. No camera, no mic — just quiet accountability that keeps everyone locked in together.

### 🏛️ Group Servers & Channels
Discord-style architecture — every group gets its own server with dedicated text and always-on voice channels. Drop in and out whenever you like, no scheduling required.

### ⏱️ Sessions & Break Timers
Schedule one-off or recurring study sessions with built-in study/break intervals and a live countdown, so your group stays synced and burnout-free.

### 🤖 AI Assessments
Tell the AI what you just learned and it generates an adaptive quiz on the spot — complete with a scored breakdown and honest, no-fluff feedback.

### 🏆 XP & Leaderboards
Real XP for real learning. Points come from sessions, assessments, and helping others — never hardcoded, always earned.

### 💬 Doubts & Chat
Ask questions, get instant AI explanations, and let your group jump in with their own replies — turning doubts into discussions.
